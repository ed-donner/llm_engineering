Riverside Cafe

Riverside Cafe has been serving coffee and light meals in the old mill district since 2011. We source our beans from a small cooperative in Guatemala and roast them in-house every Tuesday.

Our seating area overlooks the river and is open from 7am to 4pm, Monday through Saturday. We also host a monthly open-mic night on the first Friday of every month.
