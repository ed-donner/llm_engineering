## 🛡️ Vaccine Overview
*   **Common Brand Names:** Menactra, MenQuadfi, Menveo
*   **Vaccine Type:** Conjugate vaccine
*   **Administration Route:** Intramuscular (IM) injection

## 🎯 Clinical Target
Provides protective antibody levels against invasive *Neisseria meningitidis* bacteria belonging to serogroups A, C, W, and Y. Prevents rapid-onset adolescent meningococcal meningitis and life-threatening meningococcemia.

## ⚠️ Contraindications & Side Effects
*   **Contraindications:** Severe systemic allergy to prior doses or components, including diphtheria toxoid protein variations used in manufacturing.
*   **Common Side Effects:** Soreness at the injection site, transient headache, and generalized fatigue.
