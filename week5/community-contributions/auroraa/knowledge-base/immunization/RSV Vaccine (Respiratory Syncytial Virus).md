# Immunization Detail: RSV Vaccine
**Associated Members:** Older Adults (Beatrice Vance)  
**Schedule:** Single dose for adults aged 60 and older based on shared clinical decision-making

---

## 🛡️ Vaccine Overview
*   **Common Brand Names:** Arexvy, Abrysvo
*   **Vaccine Type:** Recombinant prefusion F protein vaccine (RSVPreF3)
*   **Administration Route:** Intramuscular (IM) injection

## 🎯 Clinical Target
Prevents severe Respiratory Syncytial Virus (RSV) lower respiratory tract disease (LRTD) in older adults, mitigating the risks of severe bronchitis, pneumonia, and acute hospitalization.

## ⚠️ Contraindications & Side Effects
*   **Contraindications:** History of severe hypersensitivity reactions to the vaccine components.
*   **Common Side Effects:** Local injection site pain, fatigue, headache, and myalgia.
