## 🛡️ Vaccine Overview
*   **Common Brand Names:** Prevnar 20 (PCV20), Pneumovax 23 (PPSV23)
*   **Vaccine Type:** Conjugate (PCV) or Polysaccharide (PPSV)
*   **Administration Route:** Intramuscular (IM) or Subcutaneous (SubQ) injection

## 🎯 Clinical Target
Protects against *Streptococcus pneumoniae* bacteria, the primary pathogen responsible for invasive pneumococcal disease, including bacteremic pneumonia, bacterial meningitis, and sepsis in older adults.

## ⚠️ Contraindications & Side Effects
*   **Contraindications:** Severe allergic reaction to any prior pneumococcal or diphtheria-toxoid-containing vaccine.
*   **Common Side Effects:** Erythema and swelling at the injection site, transient muscle pain, and mild fever.
