## 🛡️ Vaccine Overview
*   **Common Brand Names:** Fluzone, Fluarix, Flucelvax, Flublok
*   **Vaccine Type:** Inactivated Influenza Vaccine (IIV4) or Recombinant Influenza Vaccine (RIV4)
*   **Administration Route:** Intramuscular (IM) injection (Deltoid muscle)

## 🎯 Clinical Target
Provides active immunity against the four most prevalent circulating influenza virus strains (two Influenza A and two Influenza B subtypes) expected during the winter respiratory season. Annual reformulation is required due to rapid viral antigenic drift.

## ⚠️ Contraindications & Side Effects
*   **Contraindications:** History of severe allergic reaction (anaphylaxis) to any component of the vaccine. History of Guillain-Barré Syndrome within 6 weeks of a prior dose requires careful clinical clearance.
*   **Common Side Effects:** Local injection site soreness, low-grade fever, mild myalgia, and temporary fatigue lasting 24–48 hours.
