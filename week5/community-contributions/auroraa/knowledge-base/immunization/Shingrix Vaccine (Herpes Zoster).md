# Immunization Detail: Recombinant Zoster Vaccine (Shingles)
**Associated Members:** Living Seniors (Beatrice Vance, Richard Miller)  
**Schedule:** 2-dose series, separated by 2 to 6 months for adults aged 50 and older

---

## 🛡️ Vaccine Overview
*   **Common Brand Names:** Shingrix
*   **Vaccine Type:** Recombinant, adjuvanted suspension
*   **Administration Route:** Intramuscular (IM) injection

## 🎯 Clinical Target
Prevents Herpes Zoster (shingles) outbreaks and secondary Postherpetic Neuralgia (PHN)—the debilitating chronic nerve pain caused by the reactivation of latent varicella-zoster virus in the sensory nerve ganglia.

## ⚠️ Contraindications & Side Effects
*   **Contraindications:** Active acute shingles episode; negative test for varicella immunity (requires chickenpox vaccine series instead).
*   **Common Side Effects:** High rate of transient systemic side effects compared to other vaccines, including significant arm soreness, fatigue, headache, shivering, and low-grade fever lasting 1–3 days post-injection.
