# Immunization Detail: Varicella Virus Vaccine (Live)
**Associated Members:** Younger Generations (Chloe Vance)  
**Schedule:** 2-dose childhood sequence (Dose 1: 12–15 months, Dose 2: 4–6 years)

---

## 🛡️ Vaccine Overview
*   **Common Brand Names:** Varivax
*   **Vaccine Type:** Live attenuated virus (Oka/Merck strain)
*   **Administration Route:** Subcutaneous (SubQ) injection (Typically triceps tissue area)

## 🎯 Clinical Target
Provokes long-lasting active cellular and humoral immunity against the varicella-zoster virus, preventing chickenpox infections and minimizing breakthrough cases.

## ⚠️ Contraindications & Side Effects
*   **Contraindications:** Pregnancy, significant immunosuppression (due to active leukemia, lymphoma, HIV, or high-dose systemic steroids), or severe anaphylaxis history to neomycin/gelatin.
*   **Common Side Effects:** Injection site tenderness, mild transient maculopapular or varicelliform rash localized around the injection point within 1–3 weeks of delivery.
