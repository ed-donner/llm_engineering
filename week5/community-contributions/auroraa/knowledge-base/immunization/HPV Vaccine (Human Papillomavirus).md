## 🛡️ Vaccine Overview
*   **Common Brand Names:** Gardasil 9
*   **Vaccine Type:** Recombinant virus-like particle (VLP) vaccine
*   **Administration Route:** Intramuscular (IM) injection

## 🎯 Clinical Target
Prevents infection from 9 high-risk and low-risk strains of Human Papillomavirus (Types 6, 11, 16, 18, 31, 33, 45, 52, and 58). Extensively cuts the incidence of cervical, vulvar, vaginal, anal, and oropharyngeal cancers, as well as genital warts.

## ⚠️ Contraindications & Side Effects
*   **Contraindications:** Hypersensitivity to yeast (utilized in cellular expression processing) or severe prior reaction.
*   **Common Side Effects:** Local injection site discomfort, swelling, erythema, and a notable tendency for syncope (fainting) immediately following administration in adolescent populations.
