## 🛡️ Vaccine Overview
*   **Common Brand Names:** Adacel, Boostrix
*   **Vaccine Type:** Combination toxoid and acellular vaccine
*   **Administration Route:** Intramuscular (IM) injection

## 🎯 Clinical Target
Maintains neutralizing antibodies against *Clostridium tetani* (tetanus toxin causing lockjaw), *Corynebacterium diphtheriae* (respiratory diphtheria), and *Bordetella pertussis* (whooping cough). Crucial for preventing pertussis transmission to infants.

## ⚠️ Contraindications & Side Effects
*   **Contraindications:** History of encephalopathy within 7 days of a previous pertussis-containing vaccine.
*   **Common Side Effects:** Localized arm pain, muscle stiffness, swelling, and occasionally a hard nodule at the injection site that dissolves over several weeks.
