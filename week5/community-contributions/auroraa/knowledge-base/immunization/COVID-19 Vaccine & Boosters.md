## 🛡️ Vaccine Overview
*   **Common Brand Names:** Comirnaty (Pfizer-BioNTech), Spikevax (Moderna)
*   **Vaccine Type:** Messenger RNA (mRNA) nucleoside-modified lipid nanoparticle formulation
*   **Administration Route:** Intramuscular (IM) injection

## 🎯 Clinical Target
Induces viral spike protein synthesis in host cells to provoke neutralizing antibody and T-cell responses against SARS-CoV-2 variants, drastically decreasing severe infection, hospitalization, and mortality markers.

## ⚠️ Contraindications & Side Effects
*   **Contraindications:** Immediate or severe anaphylactic hypersensitivity to polyethylene glycol (PEG) or prior mRNA doses.
*   **Common Side Effects:** Deltoid tenderness, fatigue, headache, muscle pain, chills, and mild pyrexia. Rare risk of transient myocarditis/pericarditis primarily noted in young males.
