# Medication Detail: Lisinopril
**Associated Member:** Arthur Pendelton (Maternal Great-Grandfather - Historical)  
**Status:** Discontinued / Historical Record

---

## 💊 Drug Overview
*   **Brand Equivalents:** Zestril, Prinivil
*   **Therapeutic Class:** ACE (Angiotensin-Converting Enzyme) Inhibitor
*   **Prescribed Dosage:** 20 mg
*   **Frequency:** Once daily (Morning)

## 🎯 Clinical Indication & Target
Prescribed for chronic **Essential Hypertension** and secondary renal protection due to co-morbid Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus. The primary goal was keeping systolic pressure below 140 mmHg to limit cardiovascular strain.

## ⚠️ Known Interactions & Side Effects
*   **Interactions:** Potentiated risk of hyperkalemia (high potassium) if paired with potassium supplements or certain diuretics. 
*   **Monitored Side Effects:** Monitored for the characteristic ACE-inhibitor dry cough, dizziness, and changes in kidney filtration rates.

## 🧪 Monitoring Metrics
*   **Biometrics:** Seated blood pressure monitoring.
*   **Labs:** Periodic Serum Creatinine, Blood Urea Nitrogen (BUN), and Serum Potassium levels.
