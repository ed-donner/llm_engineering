# Medication Detail: Donepezil
**Associated Member:** Richard Miller (Maternal Grandfather)  
**Status:** Active Prescribing

---

## 💊 Drug Overview
*   **Brand Equivalents:** Aricept
*   **Therapeutic Class:** Acetylcholinesterase Inhibitor
*   **Prescribed Dosage:** 10 mg
*   **Frequency:** Once daily at bedtime

## 🎯 Clinical Indication & Target
Prescribed to manage **Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI) - Amnestic type**. It reversibly binds and inactivates acetylcholinesterase, stopping the breakdown of acetylcholine to maximize neurotransmitter availability in remaining cortical pathways.

## ⚠️ Known Interactions & Side Effects
*   **Interactions:** Anticholinergic drugs (blunt clinical effects). NSAIDs can raise risk of active gastric ulcers.
*   **Monitored Side Effects:** Cholinergic excess: nausea, diarrhea, insomnia, muscle cramps, bradycardia (slow heart rate), and vivid dreams.

## 🧪 Monitoring Metrics
*   **Biometrics:** Resting heart rate check (due to bradycardia side effect risk).
*   **Clinical:** Cognitive testing via Mini-Mental State Examination (MMSE) or Montreal Cognitive Assessment (MoCA) scores.
