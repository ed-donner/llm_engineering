# Medication Detail: Levothyroxine
**Associated Member:** Beatrice Vance (Maternal Grandmother)  
**Status:** Active Prescribing

---

## 💊 Drug Overview
*   **Brand Equivalents:** Synthroid, Levoxyl, Unithroid
*   **Therapeutic Class:** Thyroid Hormone (Synthetic T4)
*   **Prescribed Dosage:** 75 mcg
*   **Frequency:** Once daily in the morning on an empty stomach (30-60 mins before food/coffee)

## 🎯 Clinical Indication & Target
Indicated for chronic **Hypothyroidism**. Provides a synthetic replacement for endogenous T4 hormone to normalize cellular metabolism, heart rate, and temperature regulation.

## ⚠️ Known Interactions & Side Effects
*   **Interactions:** Calcium carbonate, iron supplements, and antacids (profoundly impair absorption; must be separated by 4 hours).
*   **Monitored Side Effects:** If over-dosed, can trigger hyperthyroid symptoms (palpitations, weight loss, heat intolerance, anxiety, tremors).

## 🧪 Monitoring Metrics
*   **Labs:** Thyroid Stimulating Hormone (TSH) and Free T4 levels evaluated every 6-12 months (or 6-8 weeks following any dosage adjustment).
