# Medication Detail: Cetirizine
**Associated Member:** Chloe Vance (Daughter)  
**Status:** Active Prescribing

---

## 💊 Drug Overview
*   **Brand Equivalents:** Zyrtec
*   **Therapeutic Class:** Second-Generation Antihistamine (H1 Receptor Antagonist)
*   **Prescribed Dosage:** 10 mg
*   **Frequency:** Once daily

## 🎯 Clinical Indication & Target
Prescribed for seasonal **Allergic Rhinitis** induced by tree, mold, and grass pollen. It blocks peripheral histamine H1 receptors, stopping histamine from triggering hives, watery eyes, and mucus secretion.

## ⚠️ Known Interactions & Side Effects
*   **Interactions:** Central nervous system depressants, alcohol, or sedatives can compound sleepiness.
*   **Monitored Side Effects:** Mild drowsiness (higher risk than fexofenadine/loratadine), dry mouth, and fatigue.

## 🧪 Monitoring Metrics
*   **Clinical:** Monitoring daytime attentiveness and performance during school and varsity athletic schedules.
