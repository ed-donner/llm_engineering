# Medication Detail: Finasteride
**Associated Member:** Richard Miller (Maternal Grandfather)  
**Status:** Active Prescribing

---

## 💊 Drug Overview
*   **Brand Equivalents:** Proscar
*   **Therapeutic Class:** 5-Alpha Reductase Inhibitor
*   **Prescribed Dosage:** 5 mg
*   **Frequency:** Once daily

## 🎯 Clinical Indication & Target
Prescribed for long-term reduction of **Benign Prostatic Hyperplasia (BPH)**. It inhibits the Type II 5-alpha reductase enzyme, preventing the conversion of testosterone to dihydrotestosterone (DHT) to gradually shrink prostate volume.

## ⚠️ Known Interactions & Side Effects
*   **Interactions:** No major clinical drug-drug interactions, but alters screening labs.
*   **Monitored Side Effects:** Sexual dysfunction (decreased libido, erectile issues), breast tenderness or enlargement.

## 🧪 Monitoring Metrics
*   **Labs:** Reduces baseline Prostate-Specific Antigen (PSA) markers by roughly 50%. Future PSA results must be mathematically adjusted to guarantee accurate oncological screening.
