# Medication Detail: Tamsulosin
**Associated Member:** Richard Miller (Maternal Grandfather)  
**Status:** Active Prescribing

---

## 💊 Drug Overview
*   **Brand Equivalents:** Flomax
*   **Therapeutic Class:** Alpha1a-Adrenoreceptor Antagonist
*   **Prescribed Dosage:** 0.4 mg
*   **Frequency:** Once daily, 30 minutes following the same meal each day (Bedtime preferred)

## 🎯 Clinical Indication & Target
Prescribed for **Benign Prostatic Hyperplasia (BPH)**. It selectively blocks alpha1a receptors in the prostate capsule and bladder neck, relaxing smooth muscles to fix urinary hesitation, weak stream, and nocturia.

## ⚠️ Known Interactions & Side Effects
*   **Interactions:** Co-administration with erectile dysfunction medications (PDE5 inhibitors) or antihypertensives can induce sudden orthostatic drop in blood pressure.
*   **Monitored Side Effects:** Orthostatic hypotension (dizziness upon standing), headache, and nasal congestion.

## 🧪 Monitoring Metrics
*   **Biometrics:** Orthostatic blood pressure tracking.
*   **Clinical:** American Urological Association (AUA) BPH symptom score tracking index.
