# Medication Detail: Aspirin (Low Dose)
**Associated Member:** Arthur Pendelton (Maternal Great-Grandfather - Historical)  
**Status:** Discontinued / Historical Record

---

## 💊 Drug Overview
*   **Brand Equivalents:** Bayer Low Dose, Ecotrin
*   **Therapeutic Class:** Antiplatelet / NSAID
*   **Prescribed Dosage:** 81 mg
*   **Frequency:** Once daily

## 🎯 Clinical Indication & Target
Utilized as **Stroke and Cardiovascular Disease Prophylaxis**. Low-dose aspirin irreversibly inhibits platelet cyclooxygenase-1 (COX-1), preventing platelet aggregation and minimizing clots in narrowed arterial walls.

## ⚠️ Known Interactions & Side Effects
*   **Interactions:** Other NSAIDs (ibuprofen), systemic anticoagulants (Warfarin, Eliquis), which escalate hemorrhage risks.
*   **Monitored Side Effects:** Gastric mucosal irritation, hidden gastrointestinal bleeding, easy bruising, or tinnitus.

## 🧪 Monitoring Metrics
*   **Biometrics:** Routine skin checking for unexpected or large ecchymoses (bruising).
*   **Labs:** Complete Blood Count (CBC) to watch for occult drop in hemoglobin/hematocrit.
