# Medication Detail: Sertraline
**Associated Member:** Sarah Vance (Mother)  
**Status:** Active Prescribing

---

## 💊 Drug Overview
*   **Brand Equivalents:** Zoloft
*   **Therapeutic Class:** Selective Serotonin Reuptake Inhibitor (SSRI)
*   **Prescribed Dosage:** 50 mg
*   **Frequency:** Once daily (Morning)

## 🎯 Clinical Indication & Target
Prescribed for **Generalized Anxiety Disorder (GAD)**. It blocks the presynaptic serotonin reuptake transporter (SERT), raising serotonin concentrations in synaptic clefts to help stabilize mood and reduce chronic worry.

## ⚠️ Known Interactions & Side Effects
*   **Interactions:** MAO inhibitors, linezolid (Serotonin Syndrome risk), NSAIDs/Anticoagulants (can increase risk of upper GI bleeding).
*   **Monitored Side Effects:** Nausea, initial sleep disruption, mild weight fluctuations, and dry mouth.

## 🧪 Monitoring Metrics
*   **Clinical:** Structured anxiety tracking scales (GAD-7 tool scoring).
*   **Labs:** Periodic sodium levels in older populations (risk of SIADH), though less critical at this age bracket.
