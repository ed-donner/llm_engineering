# Medication Detail: Ferrous Sulfate (Iron)
**Associated Member:** Sarah Vance (Mother)  
**Status:** Active Prescribing

---

## 💊 Drug Overview
*   **Brand Equivalents:** Feosol
*   **Therapeutic Class:** Mineral Supplement / Iron Preparation
*   **Prescribed Dosage:** 325 mg elemental equivalents
*   **Frequency:** Every other day (Taken with Vitamin C/Orange Juice to enhance assimilation)

## 🎯 Clinical Indication & Target
Prescribed to treat **Iron-Deficiency Anemia**. Provides raw iron building blocks for hemoglobin synthesis, supporting red blood cell generation to fix low systemic oxygen carrying capacity.

## ⚠️ Known Interactions & Side Effects
*   **Interactions:** Fluoroquinolones, tetracyclines, levothyroxine, and antacids (profoundly blocks their absorption; requires minimum 2-hour window).
*   **Monitored Side Effects:** Constipation, dark/black stools, nausea, or localized epigastric distress.

## 🧪 Monitoring Metrics
*   **Labs:** Serum Ferritin, Total Iron-Binding Capacity (TIBC), and Hemoglobin/Hematocrit levels checked every few months.
