# Medication Detail: Rosuvastatin
**Associated Member:** Beatrice Vance (Maternal Grandmother)  
**Status:** Active Prescribing

---

## 💊 Drug Overview
*   **Brand Equivalents:** Crestor
*   **Therapeutic Class:** HMG-CoA Reductase Inhibitor (High/Moderate Potency Statin)
*   **Prescribed Dosage:** 10 mg
*   **Frequency:** Once daily at bedtime

## 🎯 Clinical Indication & Target
Prescribed for **Primary Hypercholesterolemia**. It competitively inhibits the rate-limiting enzyme in cholesterol biosynthesis, upregulating LDL receptors on liver cells to pull circulating bad cholesterol from the bloodstream.

## ⚠️ Known Interactions & Side Effects
*   **Interactions:** Cyclosporine, gemfibrozil, and heavy antacid usage.
*   **Monitored Side Effects:** Myopathy, mild transient elevation in liver enzymes (transaminitis), and small increases in baseline blood glucose levels.

## 🧪 Monitoring Metrics
*   **Labs:** Fasting Lipid Panel (Total Cholesterol, HDL, LDL, Triglycerides) and basic metabolic chemistry screens.
