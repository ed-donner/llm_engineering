# Medication Detail: Metformin
**Associated Member:** Arthur Pendelton (Maternal Great-Grandfather - Historical)  
**Status:** Discontinued / Historical Record

---

## 💊 Drug Overview
*   **Brand Equivalents:** Glucophage, Fortamet
*   **Therapeutic Class:** Biguanide (Antidiabetic)
*   **Prescribed Dosage:** 1000 mg
*   **Frequency:** Twice daily (with breakfast and dinner)

## 🎯 Clinical Indication & Target
Prescribed for glycemic control in **Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus**. It works by decreasing hepatic glucose production, decreasing intestinal absorption of glucose, and improving insulin sensitivity. 

## ⚠️ Known Interactions & Side Effects
*   **Interactions:** Iodinated contrast dyes used during imaging (requires temporary suspension to prevent kidney strain).
*   **Monitored Side Effects:** Gastrointestinal upset (nausea, cramping, diarrhea), metallic taste, and a rare risk of lactic acidosis.

## 🧪 Monitoring Metrics
*   **Biometrics:** Daily fasting capillary blood glucose tracking.
*   **Labs:** Glycated Hemoglobin (HbA1c) every 3 to 6 months; annual Estimated Glomerular Filtration Rate (eGFR).
