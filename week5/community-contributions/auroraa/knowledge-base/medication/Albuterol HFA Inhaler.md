# Medication Detail: Albuterol HFA
**Associated Member:** Chloe Vance (Daughter)  
**Status:** Active Prescribing

---

## 💊 Drug Overview
*   **Brand Equivalents:** ProAir HFA, Ventolin HFA, Proventil HFA
*   **Therapeutic Class:** Beta2-Adrenergic Agonist (Short-Acting - SABA)
*   **Prescribed Dosage:** 90 mcg per inhalation (1-2 puffs)
*   **Frequency:** As needed (PRN) for acute bronchospasm or 15-30 minutes prior to intense soccer exercise

## 🎯 Clinical Indication & Target
Indicated as a rescue option for **Allergic Asthma** and exercise-induced bronchoconstriction. It stimulates beta2 receptors on bronchial smooth muscle, causing rapid relaxation and immediate widening of the airways.

## ⚠️ Known Interactions & Side Effects
*   **Interactions:** Beta-blockers (can blunt effectiveness and cause bronchospasm), loop diuretics (may accelerate potassium depletion).
*   **Monitored Side Effects:** Tachycardia (racing heart), muscle tremors, mild hyperactivity, and jitters.

## 🧪 Monitoring Metrics
*   **Biometrics:** Peak Expiratory Flow Rate (PEFR) home logging.
*   **Clinical:** Inhaler canister counter tracking. If used > 2 days a week for rescue, it signals a need to update baseline daily asthma control.
