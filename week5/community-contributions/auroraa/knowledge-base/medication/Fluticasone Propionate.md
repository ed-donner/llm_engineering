# Medication Detail: Fluticasone Propionate (Nasal)
**Associated Member:** Chloe Vance (Daughter)  
**Status:** Active Prescribing

---

## 💊 Drug Overview
*   **Brand Equivalents:** Flonase Allergen Relief
*   **Therapeutic Class:** Corticosteroid (Intranasal)
*   **Prescribed Dosage:** 50 mcg per spray (1 spray per nostril)
*   **Frequency:** Once daily during active seasonal allergy windows

## 🎯 Clinical Indication & Target
Indicated for severe **Allergic Rhinitis**. It works locally in the nasal mucosa to block multiple inflammatory cell types (mast cells, eosinophils, macrophages) and reduce airway swelling, sneezing, and rhinorrhea.

## ⚠️ Known Interactions & Side Effects
*   **Interactions:** Strong CYP3A4 inhibitors (ritonavir) may increase systemic corticosteroid exposure, though rare with nasal administration.
*   **Monitored Side Effects:** Nasal dryness, minor epistaxis (nosebleeds), localized irritation, and unpleasant taste/smell.

## 🧪 Monitoring Metrics
*   **Clinical:** Direct visual inspection of the nasal septum during wellness checks to monitor for mucosal drying or thinning.
