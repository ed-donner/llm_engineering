# Medication Detail: Methylphenidate ER
**Associated Member:** Ethan Vance (Son)  
**Status:** Active Prescribing

---

## 💊 Drug Overview
*   **Brand Equivalents:** Concerta, Ritalin LA
*   **Therapeutic Class:** Central Nervous System (CNS) Stimulant
*   **Prescribed Dosage:** 27 mg (Osmotic Controlled-Release Oral Delivery System)
*   **Frequency:** Once daily in the morning, immediately following a high-protein meal

## 🎯 Clinical Indication & Target
Prescribed for **Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD) - Combined Presentation**. It blocks dopamine and norepinephrine reuptake in the presynaptic neurons, increasing neurotransmitter levels in the prefrontal cortex to improve focus and impulse control.

## ⚠️ Known Interactions & Side Effects
*   **Interactions:** Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitors (MAOIs - dangerous hypertensive crisis risk), halogenated anesthetics.
*   **Monitored Side Effects:** Appetite suppression, initial insomnia, mild increase in resting heart rate/blood pressure, and minor motor tics.

## 🧪 Monitoring Metrics
*   **Biometrics:** Routine height and weight tracking mapped on pediatric growth curves to ensure no growth deceleration occurs. Checking baseline blood pressure and pulse.
*   **Clinical:** Conners parent/teacher rating scales to optimize dosing window.
