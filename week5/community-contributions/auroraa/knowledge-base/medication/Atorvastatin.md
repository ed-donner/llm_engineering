# Medication Detail: Atorvastatin
**Associated Member:** Arthur Pendelton (Maternal Great-Grandfather - Historical)  
**Status:** Discontinued / Historical Record

---

## 💊 Drug Overview
*   **Brand Equivalents:** Lipitor
*   **Therapeutic Class:** HMG-CoA Reductase Inhibitor (Statins)
*   **Prescribed Dosage:** 40 mg
*   **Frequency:** Once daily (Nightly)

## 🎯 Clinical Indication & Target
Prescribed for **Hyperlipidemia** management and aggressive secondary stroke prevention due to type 2 diabetes macrovascular risks. The goal was to heavily suppress LDL cholesterol particle creation in the liver.

## ⚠️ Known Interactions & Side Effects
*   **Interactions:** Grapefruit juice (inhibits CYP3A4 metabolism, increasing drug levels), strong CYP3A4 inhibitors (like certain antifungals).
*   **Monitored Side Effects:** Myalgia (muscle pain or weakness), mild elevations in liver enzymes, and transient digestive issues.

## 🧪 Monitoring Metrics
*   **Labs:** Liver Function Tests (ALT/AST) at baseline and during dosage updates; fasting lipid panel.
