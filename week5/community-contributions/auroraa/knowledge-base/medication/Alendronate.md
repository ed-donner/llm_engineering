# Medication Detail: Alendronate
**Associated Member:** Beatrice Vance (Maternal Grandmother)  
**Status:** Active Prescribing

---

## 💊 Drug Overview
*   **Brand Equivalents:** Fosamax
*   **Therapeutic Class:** Bisphosphonate (Bone Resorption Inhibitor)
*   **Prescribed Dosage:** 70 mg
*   **Frequency:** Once weekly (Every Sunday morning)

## 🎯 Clinical Indication & Target
Prescribed for the management of **Osteopenia** to prevent progression to structural Osteoporosis. It binds to bone surfaces and slows down osteoclast activity, allowing bone building cells to maintain skeletal architecture.

## ⚠️ Known Interactions & Side Effects
*   **Interactions:** Food, mineral water, and any other medications dramatically reduce absorption. Must be taken with plain water only, remaining completely upright for 30 minutes.
*   **Monitored Side Effects:** Severe esophageal irritation or ulceration, atypical femur fractures, and local bone/joint pain.

## 🧪 Monitoring Metrics
*   **Biometrics:** Height tracking to rule out spinal compression fractures.
*   **Labs/Imaging:** Dual-Energy X-ray Absorptiometry (DEXA) scans every 2 years.
