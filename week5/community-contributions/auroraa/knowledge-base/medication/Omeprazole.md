# Medication Detail: Omeprazole
**Associated Member:** Charles Vance (Father)  
**Status:** Active Prescribing

---

## 💊 Drug Overview
*   **Brand Equivalents:** Prilosec
*   **Therapeutic Class:** Proton Pump Inhibitor (PPI)
*   **Prescribed Dosage:** 20 mg
*   **Frequency:** Once daily, taken 30-60 minutes before the first meal of the day

## 🎯 Clinical Indication & Target
Indicated for **Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease (GERD)**. It shuts down active parietal cell hydrogen-potassium ATPase pumps in the stomach lining, suppressing gastric acid generation to let injured esophageal tissue heal.

## ⚠️ Known Interactions & Side Effects
*   **Interactions:** Decreases absorption of drugs requiring low gastric pH (ketoconazole, iron). Competes with clopidogrel processing.
*   **Monitored Side Effects:** Mild headache, abdominal pain. Long-term risks include micronutrient deficiencies (B12, Magnesium) and bone density degradation.

## 🧪 Monitoring Metrics
*   **Labs:** Long-term users are checked occasionally for serum Magnesium and Vitamin B12 counts.
*   **Clinical:** Evaluation of active breakthrough reflux symptoms or voice hoarseness.
