# Diagnostic Record: Brain MRI (Without Contrast)
**Associated Member:** Arthur Pendelton (Maternal Great-Grandfather)  
**Status:** Archive Record (Performed: 2017)  
**Ordering Provider:** Dr. Angela Rossi (St. Jude Medical Center)

---

## 🔎 Procedure & Technique
*   **Modality:** Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) 
*   **Field Strength:** 1.5 Tesla
*   **Anatomical Target:** Cranium / Encephalon

## 🎯 Clinical Indication & Objectives
Ordered following a routine senior wellness exam where the patient exhibited mild focal gait hesitation and transient memory lapses. The goal was to rule out severe normotensive hydrocephalus, intracranial masses, or acute infarctions.

## 📋 Key Findings & Observations
*   **Vascular:** Extensive, age-related white matter changes observed. Notable presence of mild chronic microhemorrhages in the basal ganglia.
*   **Structural:** Generalized cortical atrophy and ventricular enlargement matching the patient's age bracket. 
*   **Exclusions:** No evidence of midline shift, acute mass effect, or intracranial tumor development.

## 📈 Clinical Conclusion
Findings were consistent with advanced cerebral small vessel disease and age-related microvascular changes. These findings directly prompted the optimization of his antiplatelet regimen (Aspirin) and blood pressure controls.
