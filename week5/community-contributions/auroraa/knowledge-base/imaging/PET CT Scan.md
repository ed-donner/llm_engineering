# Diagnostic Record: Whole-Body PET/CT Scan
**Associated Member:** Eleanor Miller (Maternal Grandmother - Historical)  
**Status:** Archive Record (Performed: 2020)  
**Ordering Provider:** Dr. Susan Vance (Oncology)

---

## 🔎 Procedure & Technique
*   **Modality:** Positron Emission Tomography combined with Computed Tomography (PET/CT)
*   **Radiotracer Used:** Fluorodeoxyglucose (18F-FDG) intravenously injected

## 🎯 Clinical Indication & Objectives
Staging and restructuring evaluation following suspected symptomatic recurrence of primary Stage IIB Triple-Negative Breast Cancer.

## 📋 Key Findings & Hypermetabolic Mapping
*   **Skeletal:** Widespread hypermetabolic focus noted throughout the axial skeleton, with prominent osteolytic lesions visible on CT within the lumbar spine and pelvis.
*   **Hepatic:** Multiple focal areas of avid FDG uptake within both lobes of the liver, with the largest lesion measuring 2.4 cm.
*   **Thoracic:** No active internal mammary or axillary adenopathy detected.

## 📈 Clinical Conclusion
Findings confirmed a widespread systemic recurrence of her breast cancer, with extensive bone and liver metastases. These imaging results led to a transition from curative treatment to a palliative care approach.
