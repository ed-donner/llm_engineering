## 🔎 Procedure & Technique
*   **Modality:** Type III Portable Home Sleep Monitoring Device
*   **Tracked Channels:** Oronasal airflow, respiratory effort bands, pulse oximetry, and body position indexes.

## 🎯 Clinical Indication & Objectives
Diagnostic evaluation for suspected sleep-disordered breathing due to parent reports of heavy nocturnal snoring, fragmented sleep patterns, and daytime fatigue.

## 📋 Key Findings & Metrics
*   **Apnea-Hypopnea Index (AHI):** 12 events per hour of recorded sleep.
*   **Oxygen Desaturation Index (ODI):** 8 events/hr; minimum oxygen saturation remained safe at 91%.
*   **Severity Reference Intervals:**
    *   *Normal Sleep:* AHI < 5
    *   *Mild OSA:* AHI 5 – 14.9
    *   *Moderate OSA:* AHI 15 – 29.9
    *   *Severe OSA:* AHI ≥ 30

## 📈 Clinical Conclusion
Confirms **Mild Obstructive Sleep Apnea (OSA)**. Findings support non-invasive conservative treatment, including weight optimization, side-sleeping training, and minimizing nighttime GERD triggers.
